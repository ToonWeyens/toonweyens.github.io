## How I can help

### AI and data architecture

For organisations adopting AI that need an architecture which survives contact with production. I design data and AI systems around what the business actually needs to measure.

**Typical deliverables.** Architecture review and target-state design; platform and vendor evaluation against scored criteria; proof-of-concept design with success metrics agreed before the work starts; production-readiness and data-quality assessment; governance and data-trust design.

**Relevant experience.** Since 2019, enterprise AI and data platforms — MathWorks, DataRobot, Dataloop, Anomalo — leading multi-stakeholder technical evaluations for global enterprises across energy, automotive, defence, insurance, telecoms and retail.

### HPC and simulation performance

For teams whose numerical codes are too slow, too expensive, or both. Profiling, parallelisation and acceleration.

**Typical deliverables.** Performance profile and bottleneck analysis; parallelisation and GPU roadmap; feasibility assessment for ML surrogates and learned preconditioners, qualified on kernel share rather than on enthusiasm; implementation and benchmarking against a defined baseline.

**Relevant experience.** PhD in computational physics; postdoctoral Monaco Fellow at the ITER Organization; author of [PB3D](https://pb3d.github.io/), a parallel Fortran code for three-dimensional magnetohydrodynamic stability; lecturer in plasma physics and computational methods for HPC at Eindhoven University of Technology.

### Technical go-to-market for deep tech

For research-heavy companies with excellent technology and a need for professional commercial motion. Who exactly to sell to, how to qualify them, and how to run pilots that convert rather than merely impress.

**Typical deliverables.** Ideal-customer profile and qualification criteria; positioning and technical narrative; pilot and proof-of-value playbook; pipeline review and forecast discipline.

**Relevant experience.** Led a global pre-sales solutions architecture team; designed the methodology for full-cycle proofs of concept and enterprise evaluations; built the technical narrative and enablement that shortened enterprise sales cycles. Enterprise qualification frameworks (MEDDICC) and consultative technical selling, applied through procurement, security and data-governance review at global enterprises.

### Training and workshops

For technical teams, and for executives who have to make decisions about technology they do not build themselves.

**Typical formats.** Half-day and full-day workshops with hands-on labs; executive briefings; short lecture series. Topics include HPC acceleration, LLM fine-tuning and evaluation, agentic systems and their failure modes, and pragmatic AI adoption.

**Relevant experience.** University lecturer at TU/e in the BSc Applied Physics and MSc Nuclear Fusion programmes; public workshops and webinars across seven years in technical vendor roles.

## How I work

Every engagement is scoped to named deliverables with a fixed end date. You always know what you are getting.

- **Diagnostic**: two to five days, ending in a written assessment you can act on whether or not we work together afterwards.
- **Project**: defined deliverables, agreed timeline, fixed end date.
- **Advisory**: typically one to two days per week, reviewed each quarter.

Remote or on-site. Working languages: English, Dutch, Spanish (selected topics).

Rates on request, and I will tell you early if I think the work does not need me.
